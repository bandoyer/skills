code-review: sort findings in reports for a person into Act on, Consider, Noted, and Dismissed, judge findings relayed from another reviewer the same way, and name the test shapes that cannot fail for a defect. A caller's output contract takes precedence and a verified blocker is never dropped to shorten the list. diagnosing-bugs: after two failed fixes that share an assumption, test the assumption before a third fix. retro: filter candidates for durability, decision change, existing coverage, and enforceability before presenting them.
