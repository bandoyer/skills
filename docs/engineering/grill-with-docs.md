## What it does

`grill-with-docs` combines a focused design interview with maintaining the project's domain glossary and decision records. Creating those documents is part of this explicitly selected workflow.

## When to reach for it

Invoke `/grill-with-docs` when you want both the interview and durable domain documentation in a workspace. For an interview without those edits, use [grill-me](https://github.com/bandoyer/skills/blob/main/docs/productivity/grill-me.md).

## Common questions

**Does having a repository mean I must use this for every change?**

No. Choose it when the requested outcome needs unresolved domain decisions and their documentation. A clear implementation request can proceed directly.

**Will it keep asking about future features?**

No. The interview follows the current objective, selects reasonable routine defaults, and preserves the accepted scope.

## It's working if

- Material decisions become clear enough to act on.
- The glossary and decision records match what was agreed.
- The interview ends without a mandatory new implementation approval.
